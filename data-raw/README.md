Raw data

1. Global canopy height map for the year 2020 ([Lang et al., 2023](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41559-023-02206-6)):
      has 10 m ground sampling distance
      Download from ETH Zurich (https://doi.org/10.3929/ethz-b-000609802)
      stored on workstation2: http://130.92.119.132/data_2/archive/vegheight_lang_2023
2. Global Topographic Wetness Index (TWI) dataset ([Marthew et al., 2015](https://hess.copernicus.org/articles/19/91/2015/)):
      has a spatial resolution of 15 arcsec (about 450m)
      Download from [CEH Catalogue](https://catalogue.ceh.ac.uk/documents/6b0c4358-2bf3-4924-aa8f-793d468b92be)
      Stored on workstation2: http://130.92.119.132/data/archive/gti_marthews_2015
